\noindent
Time in every process-tensor diagram runs \textbf{right to left}
(\(t_n \leftarrow t_0\)).
Kets are right-pointing triangles (\texttt{----|>});
bras are left-pointing triangles (\texttt{<|----}).
Spaces are distinguished by line structure, not colour alone.

\verb|\PTIdentity| & [keys] name pos label & Identity box \(\mathcal I\). \\
\verb|\PTControl| & [keys] name pos label & General control \(\mathcal A_k\). \\
\verb|\PTUnitary| & [keys] name pos label & Unitary \(U_k\). \\
\verb|\PTPrepare| & [keys] name pos label & Preparation (triangle). \\

\verb|\PTProjective| & [keys] name pos label & Projective instrument. \\

\verb|\PTTraceHilbert| & ket-coord, bra-coord & Magenta Hilbert-space trace cup. \\
\verb|\PTTraceLiouville| & name, pos, label & Compact Liouville \(\mathrm{Tr}\) node. \\
\verb|\PTCombiner| & [dir] name pos label & Fuse \(s\otimes s'\to\ell\). \\
\verb|\PTCombinerInv| & [keys] name pos label & Inverse combiner. \\
\verb|\PTIndexLabel| & [keys] coord & Math + ITensor tag + prime. \\
\verb|\PTCup| / \verb|\PTCap| & [style] A B & Smooth cup / cap. \\
\verb|\PTTimeArrow| & [anchor] right left label & Left-pointing time arrow. \\
\verb|\PTDrawProcessMPO| & [keys] & Arbitrary-\(n\) PT-MPO. \\
\verb|\PTDrawJointProcess| & [keys] & Joint (non-MPO) process. \\
\verb|\PTOperatorLegs| & node & Ket (north) and bra (south) stubs. \\
\verb|\PTCallout| & [keys] coord text & Sparse annotation. \\
